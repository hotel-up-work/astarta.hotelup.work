# Астарта

Live site: https://astarta.hotelup.work

## About
Astarta is a hotel-restaurant complex in Kamianets-Podilskyi, Ukraine, located at Khmelnytske Shose, 36. It combines a restaurant serving Ukrainian and European cuisine, two banquet halls (capacities of up to 100 and up to 150 guests), a bar, and 7 hotel rooms, positioning itself as a single venue for weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, corporate events, and family celebrations where guests can also stay overnight after the event.

## Amenities
- Restaurant with Ukrainian and European cuisine
- Two banquet halls (up to 100 guests and up to 150 guests)
- Bar
- 7 hotel rooms for overnight stays
- Parking for guests
- Internet access
- Check-in from 14:00, check-out until 12:00
